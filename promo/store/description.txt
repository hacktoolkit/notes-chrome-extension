Page Notes is for people who think while they browse. Write a note on any page and it is there the next time you land on that page, even when the URL is different.

A NOTE THAT FOLLOWS THE PAGE
A pull request and its Files tab. A Jira issue and its board link. A LinkedIn profile and its Experience page. A Google Doc in edit and view mode. One note, with dated entries, and a count on the toolbar icon so you know the page has one.

A LOCAL RECORD OF WHAT YOU SAW
Every time you save an entry or open the popup on a noted page, Page Notes keeps what the page looked like: title, metadata and text, stored only in your browser. Pages change and people edit their profiles; your captures do not. When a page differs from your last visit, the popup says so and shows what changed.

PROFILE INSIGHTS
On a profile page, the capture is read into a work history, and Page Notes points out what a careful reader would ask about: two full-time roles that overlap, two current roles, dates that run backwards, short stints, long gaps, a stated "10+ years" the visible history does not reach, and anything that changed since your last visit. It summarises years, employers and average tenure and builds a one-line card grouped by employer, ready to paste. These are questions to ask, never verdicts.

REMIND ME, SNOOZE THAT TAB
Remind me about this page in an hour, tonight, tomorrow, next week or at a time. Snooze a tab: close it now, get it back later.

CAPTURE A QUOTE
Select text, right-click, add it to the page's note.

YOUR NOTES ARE FILES
Every note is Markdown with YAML frontmatter that Obsidian reads natively. Download them all as a zip, with every capture alongside as plain text, import them back, or connect an Obsidian vault folder and have every save written straight into it.

PRIVATE BY CONSTRUCTION
No accounts, no server, no analytics, no network requests. Nothing runs on pages you merely visit; a page is only read when you act on it. Open source under the MIT license.
